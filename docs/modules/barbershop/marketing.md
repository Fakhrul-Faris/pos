## Section: The Problem

### How many people left?

Three chairs. Four barbers. One scribbled list on the counter — and you're still cutting. Walk-ins on paper. Bookings in WhatsApp. Nobody looking at the same board.

**The paper queue**
#14 crossed out. #17 written twice. Someone went for a smoke and lost their spot. A customer asks *"Boss, how many people left?"* — you look at the paper, look at the chairs, and hope you're right. They don't wait to find out.

**WhatsApp from every direction**
"Can I come at 2?" lands in your chat. Same question in your barber's chat. "I'm outside already" — but his chair is still busy. Walk-ins keep walking in. You're guessing capacity across five phones and one notepad. Overbook and your barbers burn out. Underbook and chairs sit empty.

**Split night**
Lights off. Cash on the counter. Bank transfers on three screens. You're going down the paper list — who cut who, which beard trims got added, what each barber is owed. One number doesn't match. Now it's not closing time. It's negotiation time.

## Section: Three Screens

### Three screens. One calm shop.

No customer app. No per-barber login. No juggling WhatsApp bookings and a paper queue.

**Customer web — They book from the door**
Scan your QR. Pick a cut, a barber, a time. Get a number and a status page they can check without asking you. Name and phone — that's it.

**Counter POS — You run the chair**
One tablet, whole team. Tap the barber on duty. Mark arrived. Add a beard trim. Take payment. Switch barbers in one tap.

**Owner web — You own the day**
Services, hours, daily caps, walk-in blocks. Per-barber revenue at a glance. Print your QR. Export for your accountant.

## Section: FAQ

### Straight answers.

**Can walk-ins and online bookings share the same Saturday?**
Yes — that's the product. Bookings keep their time slot. Walk-ins fill the gaps. Neither steals from the other.

**What happens if I don't pay after the trial?**
Your shop QR stays live on **Lite** — free. One barber, 25 online bookings a month. Run a 3-chair shop? You'll want **Ocelot (RM109)**. Try full Ocelot free for 14 days — no card.

**Do I need to buy hardware from Miki?**
No. Your tablet or phone at the counter — we don't ship terminals. Tablet recommended. **Mantis** card tap needs NFC.

**Can I still take cash and my own DuitNow?**
Yes. Always **RM0 from us.** **Lite:** HitPay capped RM5k/mo. **Ocelot+:** unlimited HitPay. **Mantis+:** adds reconcile. Cash/DuitNow always work.

**Is RM109 actually worth it?**
Less than four haircuts a month. One extra QR booking covers it. **First 50 shops per city: RM89/mo locked for life.**

**Do customers need to download an app?**
No. Scan your shop QR, book in the browser. Name and phone — that's it.

**What if the Wi‑Fi dies during a rush?**
Counter keeps going. Syncs when you're back online.

**Can customers pick their barber?**
Your call. Turn it on or off.

**I'm the owner but I still cut?**
Tap yourself on the counter. Same flow as your barbers.

**What are integrated payments — required?**
**Lite:** optional — RM5k/mo cap. **Ocelot:** unlimited, no reconcile. **Mantis+:** unlimited + reconcile. Customer +2% on rail. Cash/bank QR stay RM0.
