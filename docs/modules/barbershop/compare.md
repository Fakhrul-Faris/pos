## Primary comparison — Miki vs Fresha

**Section headline:** The honest side-by-side  
**Sub:** What barbershop owners actually ask before a busy Saturday.

| | **Miki** | **Fresha** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Built for** | Barbers & salons — **walk-in + booking hybrid** | Global salons — **appointments first** |
| **Walk-in queue** | Native — take number, now serving, skip, no-show | Basic; not built for Saturday rush |
| **Pricing model** | **Flat SaaS** — RM109/mo (Ocelot, up to 4 barbers) | Per bookable staff/mo + payment fees |
| **Free trial** | **14 days** full Ocelot, no card | ~7 days |
| **Marketplace commission** | **None** — your customers, your shop | Up to ~20% on new clients from Fresha marketplace (verify on your plan) |
| **Card / online processing** | Optional via HitPay (Phase 1B) — customer +2%, merchant RM0 on rail | Card-centric; processing fees on transactions |
| **Cash at counter** | Always works · **RM0** from Miki | Supported; not the product's centre of gravity |
| **Your own DuitNow QR** | Always works · **RM0** from Miki | Not native — card/Stripe-first |
| **Per-barber revenue** | Built in — whoever is switched in on POS | Limited for chair-rental / cut-count shops |
| **Offline counter** | Queue + checkout queue locally; sync on reconnect | Cloud-dependent |
| **LHDN e-Invoice (MyInvois)** | Patriot tier / add-on (roadmap) | Not supported — manual side workflow |
| **Customer booking** | QR web page — **no app, no account** | Fresha app / web booking |
| **Setup** | BYOD — your tablet, self-serve, minutes | Self-serve; payment onboarding friction |
| **Support** | Email (Ocelot) · Priority WhatsApp (Mantis+) | Global help centre |

**Fair note:** Fresha's remaining edge is **marketplace client discovery** — if that channel drives most of your new bookings. On product craft, booking UX is no longer a moat: a focused barbershop tool can match or beat a global salon app. **Miki's durable wins are business model (flat vs fee stack), walk-in queue, and Malaysia-native rails.**

## Cost framing — why "free" booking isn't free

**Section headline:** Count the stack, not the sticker price  
**Sub:** Fresha removed its permanent free plan in 2025. Real cost = subscription + processing + marketplace — on top of a base fee that's already nearly **2× Miki Ocelot**.

### Verified Fresha pricing (Malaysia, 3 barbers — July 2026)

Sourced from Fresha pricing enquiry. USD figures geo-served; RM approximate at prevailing rate.

| Line item | Fresha (3 team members) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Base subscription** | USD 14.95 / bookable member / month |
| **3 barbers** | **USD 44.85 / month (~RM195–210)** |
| **New client commission** | **20%** on first booking (marketplace-sourced clients) |
| **Card processing** | **~2.29% + RM1.00** per transaction |
| **SMS / marketing** | Metered — fees above monthly allowance |

> Base subscription alone for 3 barbers is already ~**RM200/mo** before a single cut is processed.

### Miki pricing (same shop — 3 barbers)

| Line item | Miki (Ocelot) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Base subscription** | **RM109 / month** — up to **4 barbers**, unlimited online bookings |
| **Founding offer** | **RM89 / month** locked — first 50 barbershops per city |
| **Cash / own DuitNow** | **RM0** from Miki — always |
| **Integrated QR/card (optional)** | Customer +2% service fee; merchant receives full subtotal |
| **Marketplace commission** | **RM0** — your clients, your shop |
| **SMS** | Not in Phase 1A — no metered surprise |

> **Subscription gap:** Miki Ocelot saves **~RM86–101/mo** vs Fresha base alone — before any transaction fees. That's **~RM1,000+/year** on software alone.

### Worked example — 3-chair barbershop, busy month

*Illustrative. Adjust volumes to your shop.*

**Assumptions:** RM25,000 monthly revenue · 500 cuts · 60% paid by card/DuitNow through app · 8 new clients from Fresha marketplace @ RM45 average first visit

| Cost layer | Fresha | Miki (Ocelot) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Base subscription | ~RM200 | **RM109** |
| Marketplace commission (8 × RM45 × 20%) | ~RM72 | **RM0** |
| Card processing (RM15,000 × 2.29% + 300 txn × RM1) | ~RM643 | **RM0**† |
| **Estimated monthly total** | **~RM915** | **RM109** |

† *Miki: cash + own DuitNow = RM0. If merchant opts into HitPay for card/QR, customer pays +2% — merchant still receives full subtotal, no RM1-per-txn stack.*

**Annualised difference (this scenario):** ~**RM9,600** stayed in the shop on Miki vs Fresha — mostly from processing and commission, not subscription alone.

**One-liner:**

> Fresha earns on your revenue. Miki earns on your subscription.

**Founding offer footnote:**

> **RM89/mo locked for life** — first 50 barbershops per city. One Fresha marketplace commission on a busy Saturday can cost more than a month of Miki.

### Typical cost layers (summary)

| | Fresha | Miki |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Subscription | Per staff (~RM65–70/seat) | Flat RM109 (4 barbers) |
| Revenue tax | 20% marketplace + ~2.29%+RM1 card | RM0 on cash/DuitNow |
| Predictability | Low — bill scales with volume | High — flat SaaS |

## Walk-in Saturday — where products diverge

**Section headline:** Bookings are easy. Saturdays are hard.

**Body (short):**

Fresha was built for the appointment on Tuesday at 2pm.  
Miki was built for **Tuesday's booking and Saturday's walk-in queue** — same counter, same tablet, no stolen slots.

| Scenario | Miki | Fresha |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Customer booked online for 3pm | Slot held · marked arrived on POS | Appointment in calendar |
| Walk-in at 2:45pm | Added to queue · fills gap before 3pm | Workaround / manual |
| "Now serving #12" for waiting customers | Status page + queue board | Not a core experience |
| Barber adds beard trim mid-cut | On POS, tied to active barber | Varies |

## FAQ

**Headline:** Straight answers  
**Sub:** The questions barbers ask when comparing apps.

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Is Miki cheaper than Fresha? | **Yes — by a lot.** Fresha charges ~**RM200/mo base** for 3 barbers (USD 14.95/seat) before transaction fees. Miki Ocelot is **RM109/mo** for up to 4 barbers — and **RM0** on cash/own DuitNow. A busy shop can pay **RM500–900+/mo** on Fresha once processing and marketplace commission stack. See [worked example](/compare#cost). |
| Can I use Miki and Fresha at the same time? | Yes during trial. Don't run two booking QRs at the door — pick one customer-facing link when you switch. |
| Does Miki have a client marketplace like Fresha? | **No.** We don't take commission on your customers. You bring your own clients from IG, walk-ins, and WhatsApp. |
| Fresha's booking page looks nicer. Why Miki? | Global apps spread thin across salons, spas, and nails worldwide. Miki is **barbershop-only** — queue, counter, and booking designed together. And at ~RM200/mo base for 3 barbers plus fees, polish shouldn't cost twice the subscription of a tool built for your chair. |
| What about Booksy? | Similar category to Fresha — appointment-first, global. Same Miki wedge: walk-ins, MY payments, no commission stack. |
| How is this different from StoreHub? | StoreHub is POS for F&B/retail. We're queue OS for chairs. See [StoreHub section](#secondary-comparison--miki-vs-storehub) above. |
| What happens after 14-day trial? | Subscribe to Ocelot/Mantis/Patriot — or continue on **Lite** free (1 barber, 25 bookings/mo, QR stays live). See [`pricing-funnel.md`](pricing-funnel.md). |
