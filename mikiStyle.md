# Miki Homepage (v2)

> **Brand Principle**
>
> The homepage should sell a **belief**, not a feature.
>
> Miki isn't another booking app, POS, or business management software.
>
> Miki exists to remove the friction of running a business by bringing everything together in one place.
>
> Every section should reinforce the same message:
>
> **Stop switching. Start running.**

---

# 01. Hero

## Goal

Introduce Miki's philosophy before explaining the product.

People shouldn't think:

> "Another business software."

They should think:

> "This is exactly how running a business should feel."

---

## Headline

# Stop switching.
## *Start running.*

---

## Subheadline

Your bookings, payments, customers, and day-to-day work stay connected in one place, so you can focus on running your business instead of switching between tools.

## Primary CTA

**Start free**

---

## Secondary CTA

See how it works

# 02. Problem Section

## Goal

Show that the real problem isn't bookings or payments.

The real problem is fragmented work.

---

## Headline

# Business is already hard.
## Your software shouldn't make it harder.

---

## Subtitle

Every extra app steals a little more attention from your customers.

Bookings, payments, reports, and day-to-day work shouldn't live in different places.

Miki brings them together, so your business flows from opening to closing.

---

## Recommended Layout

```text
------------------------------------------------------
        Without Miki        |        With Miki
------------------------------------------------------
```

Avoid using:

- The Miki Way
- The Old Way

People understand "Without" and "With" immediately.

---

## Left Card

### Without Miki

❌ Customers ask if they're booked

❌ Staff switch between different apps

❌ Payments live somewhere else

❌ Closing takes longer than it should

Style:

- Muted
- Grey
- Less contrast
- Slightly smaller card

---

## Right Card

### With Miki

✅ Everyone works from the same information

✅ Customers move naturally from booking to payment

✅ Nothing gets lost between staff and owner

✅ Closing takes minutes, not hours

Style:

- Bright
- Primary color
- Slightly larger card
- More visual emphasis

---

## Design Notes

The comparison shouldn't feel like software.

It should feel like two different workdays.

One stressful.

One calm.

# Writing Style

Every section should follow these principles.

## Speak about work.

Not software.

Instead of

❌ Booking management

Say

✅ Customers book without confusion.

---

Instead of

❌ POS Integration

Say

✅ Payments happen naturally.

---

Instead of

❌ Unified dashboard

Say

✅ Everyone works from the same information.

---

## Sell outcomes.

Not features.

Bad

> One screen.

Better

> Stop switching. Start running.

---

Bad

> Hardware compatible.

Better

> Use what you already own.

---

Bad

> Unified reporting.

Better

> Closing takes minutes.

---

## Use everyday language.

Business owners don't say

- Operational efficiency
- Workflow optimization
- Platform
- Management solution

They say

- I just want it to work.
- We're always switching apps.
- Saturdays are chaos.
- Closing takes forever.
- I don't know who sold what.

Write like they talk.

---

## Brand Philosophy

Business is already complicated.

Running it shouldn't be.

Miki brings bookings, payments, customers, and day-to-day work together, so businesses spend less time switching between software and more time serving people.

---

## Core Brand Message

> **Stop switching. Start running.**

Everything on the homepage should reinforce this idea.

Not "one screen."

Not "all-in-one."

Not "business management."

Those describe software.

"Stop switching. Start running." describes the transformation Miki creates.
