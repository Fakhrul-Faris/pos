## FAQ

### Do I need to buy hardware or sign a contract?

No. Miki runs on the phone or tablet you already have — no terminal, no bundle, no contract. Free for 14 days, no card required.

### How long does it take to set up?

Minutes. Create an account, add your services, and you are ready to take walk-ins and bookings.

### How do I get paid?

Payments settle straight to your bank via DuitNow. You can also take cash at the counter — Miki tracks it either way.
